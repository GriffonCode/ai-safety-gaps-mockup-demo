The Coverage Gap

Technical alignment and interpretability have 10-15 organizations each. But corporate governance, incident response, liability frameworks, and post-deployment monitoring have zero to one. These aren't obscure topics - they're how safety gets implemented in practice.

What This Means

The field has heavily invested in upstream technical research (making AI safe before deployment) but underinvested in downstream implementation (what happens after deployment, who's liable, how incidents are handled).

This mirrors early cybersecurity, which also started with technical research before developing the operational and commercial infrastructure that makes security work in practice.
