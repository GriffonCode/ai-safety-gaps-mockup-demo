Key Insight: Right-Side Gap

The right side of the matrix (Withstand, Adapt & Thrive) shows sparse coverage. The field concentrates on preventing and controlling AI risks, but has underinvested in responding when things go wrong and adapting to a world with powerful AI.

This echoes cybersecurity's evolution: early focus was on prevention, with incident response and resilience emerging later.
