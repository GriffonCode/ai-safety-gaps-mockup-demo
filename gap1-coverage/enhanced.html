The Coverage Gap

Technical alignment and interpretability have 8-12 organizations each. But corporate governance, incident response, liability frameworks, and post-deployment monitoring appear to have zero to one. These aren't obscure topics - they're how safety gets implemented in practice.

Caveat: Our search may miss organizations working under different names or doing this work internally. If you know of work we've missed, please let us know.

What This Means

The field appears to have heavily invested in upstream technical research (making AI safe before deployment) but underinvested in downstream implementation (what happens after deployment, who's liable, how incidents are handled).

This mirrors early cybersecurity, which also started with technical research before developing the operational and commercial infrastructure that makes security work in practice.
