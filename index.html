Strategic Gaps in AI Safety

The AI safety field has grown rapidly, but we lack systematic understanding of where effort is concentrated, which pathways to impact are working, and what commercial infrastructure is missing. These three analyses identify structural gaps that funders and strategists should address.

Gap 1: Field Coverage

We don't systematically track who's working on what. Some areas have 15+ organizations; others have zero coverage.

Gap 2: Pathways to Impact

Research exists, but translation to decisions is unclear. Some pathways work; others are broken or missing entirely.

Gap 3: Commercial Infrastructure

Cybersecurity has insurers, auditors, certifications. AI safety has almost none. This commercial layer is missing.

Methodology

This analysis draws on:

  • AI Safety Ontology: 115 topics mapped across 5 domains
  • Organization mapping: 28+ organizations categorized by focus
  • SPAR project analysis and funder priority review
  • Comparison with mature fields (cybersecurity, financial compliance)
