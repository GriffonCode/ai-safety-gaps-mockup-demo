Gap 2: Pathways to Impact

Research exists, but does it reach the people who make decisions? Some pathways work. Others are broken or missing entirely.

The Impact Pathway Model

For research to matter, it must flow through four stages:

RESEARCH
Who produces knowledge?
CONSUMER
Who uses it?
ACTION
What do they do?
OUTCOME
What changes?

Three Examples

Example 1: Capability Evaluations (Relatively Clear)

Research
METR, ARC, Apollo
Consumer
Labs, UK/US AISIs
Action
Delay/modify release?
Outcome
Fewer dangerous releases?

Clear buyers exist (labs, governments). But adoption is voluntary - no mandate requires acting on eval results.

Example 3: Corporate Safety Integration (Pathway Missing)

Research
Almost none
Consumer
Enterprise deployers
Action
Internal policies
Outcome
Safer deployed systems

Demand exists - enterprises want to deploy AI safely. But almost no one researches how to help them. The market exists; the supply doesn't.

The Pathway Gap

We fund research without mapping how it reaches decisions. Some pathways are well-defined (evals to labs). Others have significant investment but unclear consumers (interpretability). Others have clear demand but no supply (corporate integration).

To Strengthen This Analysis

  • Validate crucial pathways: Interview practitioners on 3-5 high-stakes pathways (e.g., evaluations to deployment decisions)
  • Estimate impact: Which pathways, if strengthened, would most reduce AI risk?
  • Identify breaks: Where do pathways fail? Missing consumers, unclear actions, or no feedback?
